I am an AI Scientist at Knorr-Bremse, building scalable hierarchical and machine-learning time-series forecasting pipelines. My work brings modern forecasting methods into dependable, large-scale industry applications.

I completed doctoral research in Industrial Engineering and Operations Research (IEOR) at IIT Bombay, advised by Prof. Narayan Rangaraj and Prof. Nandyala Hemachandra. My research focused on dependence-preserving multivariate and probabilistic hierarchical time-series forecasting.

I co-founded [ORMLAI Labs](https://www.ormlai.com/), an IIT Bombay alumni initiative delivering Operations Research, Machine Learning, and AI solutions for MSMEs and retailers.

My research and engineering interests include time-series architectures (patch-based linear models, low-rank attention Transformers, and channel-compressed pretraining), hierarchical and probabilistic forecasting, sequence classification, anomaly detection, OCR automation, and mathematical optimization.
